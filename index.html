01Welcome to SPMRP Staff

Welcome to the Saint Paul Minnesota Roleplay (SPMRP) Staff Team. A Trail Moderator (TM) is a trial-based staff member whose professionalism, activity, judgment, and knowledge of the rules are evaluated before they may be considered for full Moderator.

This handbook explains the expected standards. Follow it alongside the current SPMRP rules, Roblox Community Standards, and instructions from authorized staff leadership.

02The Trail Moderator Role

Trail Moderators help keep sessions organized and welcoming while learning SPMRP's moderation procedures.

  • Observe sessions and identify possible rule violations.
  • Answer player questions and explain rules calmly.
  • Collect factual evidence and submit incident reports.
  • Help de-escalate minor conflicts when appropriate.
  • Refer serious, repeated, unclear, or disputed cases to a full Moderator or higher.
  • Accept feedback and learn from mistakes.

A TM is not automatically a full Moderator. Your title does not grant permissions that leadership has not assigned.

03Staff Code of Conduct

Professionalism

Treat players and staff respectfully, even when someone is upset or uncooperative.

Fairness

Apply the same written rules to friends, strangers, civilians, department members, and staff alike.

Integrity

Never fabricate evidence, exaggerate claims, falsely accuse a player, or conceal misconduct to protect a friend.

Confidentiality

Keep staff discussions, private reports, and restricted evidence within authorized channels. Never share passwords or another person's sensitive personal information.

Accountability and activity

Own your mistakes, follow the team's leave procedure, and communicate if you cannot meet activity expectations.

Community safety

Harassment, bullying, discrimination, threats, exploitation, and other prohibited conduct are not acceptable. Follow Roblox Community Standards as well as SPMRP rules.

Never use a command just because it is available. If your configured permissions or leadership's instructions are stricter, follow the stricter rule.

05Moderation Procedure

  1. Observe: Find out what actually happened before reaching a conclusion.
  2. Verify: Review available chat, evidence, context, and player statements.
  3. Identify the rule: Know which written SPMRP rule may have been broken. Ask for guidance if unsure.
  4. Respond proportionately: A minor first-time issue may call for a reminder. Serious or repeated issues should be documented and escalated.
  5. Record: Note the username, time, context, evidence, rule concern, and action taken.
  6. Escalate: Refer anything beyond your authority or confidence to a full Moderator or senior staff member.

06Roleplay Enforcement

SPMRP aims to provide immersive civilian life and public safety roleplay, including Law Enforcement, Fire & EMS, and Dispatch. Enforce the written rules, not your personal preferences.

  • Fail roleplay or unrealistic actions that violate SPMRP policy.
  • Random deathmatching or intentional disruption, where prohibited.
  • Metagaming or powergaming, where prohibited.
  • Vehicle misuse and deliberate interference with a scene.
  • Disrupting emergency services or repeatedly trolling/spamming.
  • Impersonating staff or misusing staff authority.

Consider context before taking action. A confusing situation or an unproven report is not, by itself, proof that a player broke a rule.

07Handling Player Reports

  1. Let the reporting player explain the concern.
  2. Ask for relevant details without encouraging arguments.
  3. Review evidence and context when available.
  4. Hear the other side when appropriate.
  5. Record the report and refer it to the right staff member.
  6. Do not promise a punishment before the review is complete.

08When to Escalate

Contact a full Moderator or higher-ranking staff member when:

  • A player repeatedly breaks rules after intervention.
  • A case involves serious harassment, threats, or discrimination.
  • A staff member may be abusing permissions.
  • Evidence is disputed or the applicable rule is unclear.
  • A player appeals a significant moderation action.
  • The situation exceeds your permissions or training.
  • You have a conflict of interest, such as being personally involved or close friends with the person reported.

If someone appears to be in immediate danger or shares sensitive personal information, notify appropriate leadership and follow platform safety procedures. Do not repost private information.

10Abuse of Power & Misconduct

The following may lead to review, suspension of permissions, removal from the TM role, or further discipline depending on severity and evidence:

  • Using permissions for revenge, favoritism, or personal gain.
  • Threatening players to win an argument.
  • Falsifying, altering, or hiding evidence.
  • Sharing restricted staff information without authorization.
  • Protecting friends from valid reports or targeting people unfairly.
  • Using commands without authorization or claiming powers you do not have.
  • Harassing, bullying, or discriminating against community members.
  • Repeatedly ignoring legitimate staff instructions.

Reports of staff misconduct should be reviewed by an appropriate staff member who is not directly involved in the incident.

11Trial Evaluation

Leadership may assess your activity, rule knowledge, evidence quality, communication, judgment, consistency, teamwork, use of permissions, and response to feedback.

  • Pass: Meets expectations and may be considered for full Moderator.
  • Extension: Needs more time, coaching, or training.
  • Removal: Does not meet expectations or commits a serious policy violation.

Promotion is not guaranteed and remains the decision of authorized SPMRP leadership.

12TM Readiness Checklist

  • □ I understand the current SPMRP rules.
  • □ I know when to escalate an incident.

  • □ I use only permissions I have been granted.
  • □ I understand the difference between a reminder and an official punishment.
  • □ I protect confidential staff information.
  • □ I accept feedback and treat players fairly.
